Rave Dice

By Sheila Daghouti Jaatit and Kevin Jabro

About the project

Rave Dice is a game where you roll physical dice. Each dice has a micro:bit inside it. When you roll, the same number that the die shows also appears on the computer screen, with sounds and colours inspired by a rave.

The first player to reach 30 points wins. After each roll you can choose to "stay and bank" your points, or keep rolling and take a chance. But be careful: if you roll a 1, you go bust and lose your points for that round!

About us

We are Sheila Daghouti Jaatit and Kevin Jabro. We are students at Södertörn University, studying the IMD programme. We enjoy being creative and trying out new things.
